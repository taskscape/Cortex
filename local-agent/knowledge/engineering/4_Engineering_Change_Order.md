# ENGINEERING CHANGE ORDER (ECO)

**ECO Number:** ECO-2026-045  
**Date:** July 21, 2026  
**Product/Component:** Motor Controller Board V2.1  

### 1. Reason for Change
Component obsolescence. The currently used capacitor (C14) is no longer being manufactured by the supplier, causing supply chain delays.

### 2. Description of Change
Replace capacitor C14 (Part No. CAP-100uF-16V-A) with a direct equivalent (Part No. CAP-100uF-16V-B) from an alternative supplier (Vishay).

### 3. Impact Analysis
*   **Cost Impact:** Minor ($0.02 increase per unit).
*   **Schedule Impact:** None.
*   **Performance Impact:** No change to electrical characteristics or thermal performance. Verified via lab testing (Test Report #TR-889).
