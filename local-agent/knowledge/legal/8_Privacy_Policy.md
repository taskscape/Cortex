# PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date: July 21, 2026**

**SaaS-CloudApp** ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and share your information.

### 1. Information We Collect
- **Personal Data:** We may collect your name, email address, phone number, and billing information when you register for an account.
- **Usage Data:** We automatically collect information on how the Services are accessed and used (e.g., IP address, browser type, pages visited).

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data to:
- Provide, operate, and maintain our Services.
- Process your payments and manage your account.
- Send administrative information, updates, and security alerts.
- Analyze usage trends to improve our platform.

### 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. We may share your data with trusted third-party service providers (such as payment processors and cloud hosting providers) strictly for the purpose of operating our business.

### 4. Your Rights
Depending on your location (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. Please contact us at privacy@saas-cloudapp.com to exercise these rights.

### 5. Security
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, though no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.
