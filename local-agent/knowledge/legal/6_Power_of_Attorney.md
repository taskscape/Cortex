# GENERAL POWER OF ATTORNEY

**KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS**, that I, **Robert Johnson**, residing at 101 Maple Street, Chicago, IL 60601 (the "Principal"), hereby appoint **Emily Johnson**, residing at 202 Oak Avenue, Chicago, IL 60602 (the "Agent" or "Attorney-in-Fact"), to act in my name and on my behalf.

### 1. Grant of Authority
I grant my Agent full power and authority to act for me in any lawful way with respect to the following subjects:
- Real property transactions.
- Tangible personal property transactions.
- Stock and bond transactions.
- Banking and other financial institution transactions.
- Operation of entity or business.
- Tax matters.

### 2. Effective Date
This Power of Attorney shall become effective immediately upon my signing it. 

### 3. Durability
This Power of Attorney shall not be affected by my subsequent disability or incapacity.

### 4. Revocation
I may revoke this Power of Attorney at any time by providing written notice to my Agent.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, I have executed this Power of Attorney on **July 21, 2026**.
