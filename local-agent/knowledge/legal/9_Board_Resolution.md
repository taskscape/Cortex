# WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

**Company:** Quantum Dynamics Corporation  
**Date:** July 21, 2026  

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors of Quantum Dynamics Corporation, a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), do hereby adopt the following resolutions by written consent in lieu of a meeting:

**WHEREAS**, the Board of Directors deems it to be in the best interests of the Company to enter into a commercial lease agreement for new office space located at 500 Corporate Center Drive, Austin, TX.

**NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED**, that the CEO and CFO of the Company are hereby authorized and directed to negotiate, execute, and deliver the lease agreement on behalf of the Company, along with any other documents necessary to effectuate the transaction.

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that any and all actions previously taken by the officers of the Company in connection with the aforementioned lease are hereby ratified, confirmed, and approved in all respects.

This Unanimous Written Consent may be executed in counterparts and shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board of Directors.
