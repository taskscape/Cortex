# INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is made as of **July 21, 2026**, between **DesignWorks Studio** ("Client") and **Sarah Jenkins** ("Contractor").

### 1. Services Provided
Contractor agrees to provide freelance graphic design services, specifically creating a new branding package and website UI mockups (the "Services").

### 2. Payment
Client agrees to pay Contractor a flat fee of **$8,500** for the Services. Payment will be made in two installments: 50% upfront upon signing this Agreement, and 50% upon final delivery and approval of the designs.

### 3. Relationship of the Parties
Contractor is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employer-employee relationship, partnership, or joint venture. Contractor is responsible for their own taxes and benefits.

### 4. Intellectual Property
Upon full payment, all designs, mockups, and final deliverables created by Contractor under this Agreement shall become the sole property of the Client as "work made for hire."

### 5. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement with 14 days written notice. In the event of termination, Client shall pay Contractor for all Services satisfactorily performed up to the termination date.
