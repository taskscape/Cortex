# EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into on **July 21, 2026**, between **Acme Corporation** ("Employer") and **Jane Doe** ("Employee").

### 1. Position and Duties
The Employer agrees to employ the Employee as **Senior Software Engineer**. The Employee agrees to perform all duties and responsibilities associated with this role to the best of their abilities.

### 2. Compensation
**Base Salary:** The Employer shall pay the Employee an annual base salary of **$140,000**, payable in bi-weekly installments.
**Bonus:** The Employee is eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to 15% of the base salary.

### 3. Benefits
The Employee shall be entitled to participate in all employee benefit plans offered by the Employer, including health insurance, 401(k) matching, and 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year.

### 4. At-Will Employment
The Employee's employment with the Employer is "at-will." This means that either the Employer or the Employee may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause, and with or without notice.

### 5. Confidentiality
The Employee agrees not to disclose any confidential information or trade secrets of the Employer during or after the term of employment.
