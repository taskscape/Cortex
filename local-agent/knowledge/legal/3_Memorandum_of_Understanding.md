# MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU)

**Date:** July 21, 2026

**BETWEEN:**
**Alpha Logistics LLC** (Hereinafter "Alpha")
**AND**
**Beta Manufacturing Inc.** (Hereinafter "Beta")

### 1. Objective
The purpose of this MOU is to establish a framework for collaboration between Alpha and Beta regarding the optimization of the supply chain operations in the European market.

### 2. Roles and Responsibilities
- **Alpha:** Will provide advanced logistics tracking software and dedicated freight forwarding services.
- **Beta:** Will exclusively use Alpha's software for all outbound European shipments and provide necessary API access to their inventory systems.

### 3. Financial Arrangements
This MOU does not create any financial obligations between the parties. Specific financial terms will be detailed in a separate definitive agreement.

### 4. Non-Binding Nature
This MOU is a statement of intent and does not constitute a legally binding contract, except for the confidentiality provisions contained herein.

### 5. Term and Termination
This MOU is valid for a period of twelve (12) months from the date of signing and may be terminated by either party with a 30-day written notice.
