# SPONSORSHIP ROADMAP

**Project:** Global Sustainability Initiative (Zero Waste 2030)  
**Primary Sponsor:** Elena Rostova, Chief Operating Officer (COO)  

### 1. Role of the Sponsor
The sponsor is required to be active and visible throughout the project lifecycle, build a coalition of support among other executives, and communicate directly with employees.

### 2. Activity Roadmap

**Phase 1: Project Initiation (Months 1-2)**
*   [ ] Record a 3-minute video explaining the vision and importance of Zero Waste 2030.
*   [ ] Host a leadership alignment session with VP-level stakeholders to secure budget and commitment.

**Phase 2: Implementation (Months 3-6)**
*   [ ] Send monthly email updates celebrating quick wins (e.g., elimination of single-use plastics in the cafeteria).
*   [ ] Attend at least two departmental "Green Team" meetings to show visible support.
*   [ ] Resolve escalated resource conflicts between operations and the sustainability task force.

**Phase 3: Reinforcement (Months 7+)**
*   [ ] Present the initiative's progress at the Annual Shareholder Meeting.
*   [ ] Hand out internal "Sustainability Champion" awards at the end-of-year gala.
