# CHANGE MANAGEMENT PLAN

**Project:** CRM Cloud Migration  
**Document Owner:** Change Management Office (CMO)  
**Version:** 1.0  

### 1. Executive Summary
This document outlines the strategy for managing the human and organizational impacts of migrating our on-premise CRM to a cloud-based solution.

### 2. Change Management Methodology
We will utilize the ADKAR Model (Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement) to guide employees through the transition.

### 3. Scope of Change
*   **In Scope:** Sales and Customer Support teams (200 users). Transition to new UI and processes.
*   **Out of Scope:** Marketing automation systems.

### 4. Key Milestones
1.  **Month 1:** Announce change and establish change champion network.
2.  **Month 2:** Conduct gap analysis and user readiness assessments.
3.  **Month 3:** Execute training and user acceptance testing (UAT).
4.  **Month 4:** Go-Live and launch reinforcement surveys.

### 5. Resource Allocation
*   1x Dedicated Change Manager
*   2x Training Specialists
*   10x Departmental Change Champions
