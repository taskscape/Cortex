# TRAINING PLAN

**System:** Workday HCM Implementation  
**Audience:** All Staff, HR Admins, Managers  

### 1. Training Objectives
Ensure all employees can navigate the new HR portal, request time off, and update personal details. Enable managers to approve requests and conduct performance reviews.

### 2. Training Delivery Methods
*   **eLearning Modules:** Self-paced, mandatory for all staff (30 mins).
*   **Instructor-Led Training (ILT):** Deep-dive sessions for HR Admins (4 hours).
*   **Quick Reference Guides (QRGs):** 1-page PDF cheat sheets hosted on the intranet.

### 3. Curriculum Schedule

| Module | Target Audience | Format | Deadline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Navigating the Dashboard | All Staff | eLearning | Oct 15, 2026 |
| Managing Your Team | People Managers | Zoom Webinar | Oct 20, 2026 |
| Advanced Reporting | HR Admins | ILT (In-Person) | Oct 25, 2026 |

### 4. Evaluation
End-of-module quizzes requiring an 80% passing score. Post-training surveys will measure user confidence levels.
