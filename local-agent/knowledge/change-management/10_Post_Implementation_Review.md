# POST-IMPLEMENTATION REVIEW (PIR)

**Project:** Office Relocation to Downtown HQ  
**Review Date:** July 21, 2026 (60 days post-move)  

### 1. Success Criteria Evaluation
*   **Objective 1: Complete move with zero days of operational downtime.** 
    *   *Result:* **Achieved.** IT systems were fully functional by 6:00 AM Monday.
*   **Objective 2: Maintain employee satisfaction rating of >80% regarding workspace.**
    *   *Result:* **Missed.** Survey indicates 68% satisfaction. Primary complaints are noise levels in the open-plan layout and insufficient parking.

### 2. Lessons Learned
*   **What went well:** The physical logistics (movers, packing crates) were perfectly coordinated. The welcome kits on desks were highly appreciated.
*   **What could be improved:** We under-communicated the new parking permit process, leading to confusion on day one. We also underestimated the acoustics of the new building.

### 3. Action Items (Next Steps)
*   Procure and install sound-dampening acoustic panels in the engineering wing by August 15. (Owner: Facilities)
*   Negotiate overflow parking rates with the adjacent municipal garage. (Owner: HR)
*   Formally close the relocation project and release the project team. (Owner: PMO)
