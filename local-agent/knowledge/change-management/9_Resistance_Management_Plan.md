# RESISTANCE MANAGEMENT PLAN

**Project:** Mandatory Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Rollout  
**Date:** July 21, 2026  

### 1. Anticipated Resistance
*   **Who:** Field Sales Representatives.
*   **What:** Refusal to install authenticator apps on personal mobile devices.
*   **Why (Root Cause):** Privacy concerns, inconvenience during client meetings, feeling that IT is overreaching.

### 2. Proactive Mitigation Tactics
*   **Policy Clarification:** Publish clear guidelines stating that the authenticator app does not grant IT access to personal data, photos, or location.
*   **Alternative Solutions:** Offer physical hardware tokens (e.g., YubiKey) for employees who strictly refuse to use personal devices.
*   **Grace Period:** Allow a 30-day voluntary opt-in period with incentives before making it mandatory.

### 3. Reactive Management (If resistance persists)
*   **Step 1:** Direct Manager intervention. Managers will be provided with talking points to address privacy concerns 1-on-1.
*   **Step 2:** IT support prioritization. Ensure field sales get priority routing for 2FA lockouts so their commissions are not impacted.
*   **Step 3:** Escalation to HR for non-compliance after the final cut-off date.
