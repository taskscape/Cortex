# ORGANIZATIONAL READINESS ASSESSMENT

**Project:** Agile Methodology Adoption  
**Survey Date:** July 10, 2026  
**Respondents:** 150/180 Software Engineers (83% response rate)  

### 1. Executive Summary
The engineering department shows a moderate level of readiness for adopting the Agile framework. While there is a strong desire to improve delivery speed, there is significant anxiety regarding new roles (Scrum Master, Product Owner) and meeting cadence.

### 2. Key Findings
*   **Awareness:** 90% understand *why* the change is happening.
*   **Desire:** 65% are willing to support the change; 20% are neutral; 15% are resistant.
*   **Knowledge:** Only 30% feel they have the necessary knowledge of Agile principles to succeed.

### 3. Identified Gaps
*   Lack of clarity on how legacy QA processes fit into two-week sprints.
*   Fear of micromanagement due to Daily Standup meetings.

### 4. Recommendations
1.  Launch "Agile 101" foundational training immediately.
2.  Deploy external Agile Coaches for the first three sprint cycles.
3.  Have leadership publicly address the purpose of Daily Standups (alignment, not micromanagement).
